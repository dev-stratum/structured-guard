# Code of conduct

## Our standard

This project is a place for useful technical work. Everyone taking part is
expected to be respectful and to assume good faith. Examples of good behaviour:

- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on the problem rather than the person;
- being patient with people who are new to the project or to open source;
- respecting other people's privacy, including not trying to identify or
  contact anyone outside the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks;
- publishing someone's private information, including attempts to identify a
  pseudonymous contributor or maintainer;
- spam, advertising or promotion that has nothing to do with the project;
- anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may
block people who break these rules. To report a problem privately, use the
repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, and say
that it is a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Scope

These rules apply in the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other space where someone represents the project.
